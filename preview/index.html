The story

From the Hut
to the Alhambra

2004

The Hut in Rosyth

Brothers Fraser and Craig Penman, Gec Livingstone and Gaz Louch start a band in a soundproofed hut at the bottom of the Penman family garden, known to everyone as The Hut.

2005

First gig

The Yard, Rosyth, May 2005. The banner was homemade. The noise was not small.

2006

Garden City

The first EP, recorded in The Hut. It gets them to Alan McGee's Death Disco at Notting Hill Arts Club and the Cavern Club in Liverpool.

2006

EMI, London

Three tracks recorded at EMI's London studios. A sold-out London show supporting Louis XIV, and a tip as a band to watch in 2007.

2007

Austin, Texas

South by Southwest, 14 to 18 March. From The Hut to one of the world's biggest music festivals. Then a packed T Break tent at T in the Park.

2008

The 'final' gig

Fraser takes a job offshore. Around 1,000 people pack the Alhambra in Dunfermline to say goodbye. It doesn't stick.

2009

Back again

A year later they make their comeback with their 2 Point 1 EP and a big night at Velocity.

2010

Five-piece

Cal Nicol joins on keys. Heart Attack lands, and the band plays the T Break stage at T in the Park on Saturday 10 July.

2012

Should've Known Better

The debut album, out 2 April 2012 on Townsend Records. It makes the Official Scottish Albums Chart.

2013

Save the Pars

After a year off, the band reunites for fundraising gigs at PJ Molloys to help save Dunfermline Athletic.

2015
The last one

Weekend Revolution

The final gig, at the Weekend Revolution festival in Dunfermline, at Lourenzo's. Then nothing for 11 years.

2026

Garden City, at last

Twenty years after that first EP, Garden City finally gets a proper release on 18 September 2026.

2026

Back at the Alhambra

Saturday 3 October 2026. The first hometown show in 11 years. Sold out.
